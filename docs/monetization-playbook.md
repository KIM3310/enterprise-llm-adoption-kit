# Monetization Playbook - enterprise-llm-adoption-kit

This file is a commercial planning artifact, not a guarantee of revenue. Use it to make the repository legible as a sellable product, pilot, workshop, license, or proof-of-value package.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** B2B enterprise AI governance
- **Primary buyer:** Enterprise AI, IT governance, security, platform, and operations teams.
- **Revenue wedge:** Governance toolkit with RBAC, redaction, prompt-injection checks, evals, and rollout controls.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Paid adoption workshop** - pricing hypothesis: $8k-$20k workshop
2. **Governance starter implementation** - pricing hypothesis: $25k-$80k implementation
3. **Security/eval pack subscription** - pricing hypothesis: $5k-$20k/month advisory

## Acquisition Channels

- Executive workshop deck
- Security questionnaire response pack
- PoC success criteria generator

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- make verify passes
- Sales docs are current
- Governance controls are demonstrable

## Risk Boundaries

- Synthetic data by default
- Customer adapters need scoped auth
- No compliance certification claims without audit

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Package 30/60/90 rollout
- **60 days:** Create vertical-specific demos
- **90 days:** Sell one governance workshop

## Metrics That Matter

- Policy coverage
- Eval pass rate
- Adoption readiness score

## Positioning Note

Lead with the specific operational pain, then show the runnable proof, then offer a narrow pilot. Do not pitch the repository as a vague app; pitch it as a concrete workflow with evidence, risk boundaries, and a next buying step.
