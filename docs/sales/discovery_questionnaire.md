# Discovery Questionnaire

## Business Goals
- What business outcomes are you targeting with LLM adoption?
- Which teams will own operations and support?

## Data & Security
- What data classes are in scope (PII, PHI, confidential)?
- Required retention and audit policies?
- Required access control model (RBAC, ABAC)?

## Use Cases
- Top two workflows to prioritize for PoC?
- Required latency and response quality?

## Integration
- Which IdP (OIDC/SAML) is standard?
- Logging/observability stack (Prometheus, Datadog)?
- Existing runbooks or knowledge base formats?

## Evaluation
- Do you have golden datasets or evaluation criteria?
- What are acceptance thresholds for quality and safety?

## Operations
- Expected daily volume and concurrency?
- Cost constraints and budgeting?
